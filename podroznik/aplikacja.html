Tripwillow – travel planner for Android

Keep your whole trip in one place: offline tickets with QR and boarding-pass codes, day-by-day plan with a map, packing lists, budget and offline currency converter, a travel journal, driver cards and phrasebook. Optionally, the app can find booking confirmations in your Gmail (read-only) and suggest adding them to your trips – messages are read on your phone only and never sent to any server. See the Privacy Policy.
